A tailor-made gastronomic activity

The sushi course is fully interactive and you will be able to enjoy a gastronomic activity with a first class chef. In addition, you will be able to discuss the whole experience with your colleagues at a dinner or lunch at the end of the sushi workshop.

Fun and the desire to have a good time are the basis of the course and, of course, enjoy a good dinner in pure Japanese style.

SUSHI FRESH! Japanese cuisine in the freshest and most original sushi workshop in Barcelona.

It is a sushi workshop in Barcelona, contest format and in which you will have a great time learning the techniques of this ancient cuisine. In addition, you will have to put your team creativity into practice because there will only be one winner: the one who gets the roll with the coolest design. Ideal for beginners in the learning of this Japanese cuisine.

We will welcome you with a welcome drink and introduce the cooking team who will explain the dynamics of the course.
