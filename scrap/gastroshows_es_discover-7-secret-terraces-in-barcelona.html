por Esther Fernández | Abr 19, 2023 | plans barcelona , restaurants barcelona | 0 Comentarios

You already know that at Gastroshows we love the clandestine. We love anything with a surprise effect. Therefore, we wanted to share with you these 7 terraces in Barcelona. Apart from pleasantly surprising you, they will become one of your must-visits to disconnect after work, on a weekend, alone or with company, etc. Also for special occasions. That is, any plan is perfect in these oases of peace. And I’m sure that, like us, you love Barcelona, but although we are used to its hustle and bustle, we need moments of disconnection, so you already know seven more! And if you want to live a secret gastronomic experience, we are waiting for you at La cena clandestina .

Hidden in the park of Montjuïc and surrounded by gardens with the city at its feet, the terrace of La Font del Gat is the main charm of this small and informal café-restaurant that many people in Barcelona do not know. Open from Tuesday to Sunday at noon (menu of the day) and in the evening they are open for groups. They have an original menu and a surprisingly sophisticated cuisine . In addition, it is a terrace with history. Its building was built by the famous architect Josep Puig i Cadafalch and a few meters from the Font del Gat.
