Caelis is the restaurant of the Ohla Barcelona hotel . The French chef Romain Fornell , at the helm, proposes a Catalan cuisine with Gallic roots based on seasonal products accompanied by different pairings. A culinary experience for the diner due to, among other factors, its flavors and its open kitchen.

From Wednesday to Saturday from 13:30h to 15:30h , they offer the ‘Caelis Menu’ for 42 euros (VAT included) . It consists of: a starter (to choose between two), a main course (to choose between two) followed by a selection of cheeses or a sweet dessert , plus a coffee or tea . The menu also includes one of two wines (either white or red) suggested by the sommelier. Not valid for groups of more than 12 people.
